Rotate turns pages that are sideways or upside down. The change is saved into the document.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**. Select the pages to turn in the page grid, or leave none selected to turn the page you were reading.
2. In the **Rotate** group, choose **Rotate 90°** to turn them a quarter turn clockwise.
3. For a half or three-quarter turn, choose **Rotate 180°** or **Rotate 270°**, beside it. In a narrower window some of them are under the group's **More**.

![The Organize section's Rotate group with Rotate 90°, Rotate 180° and Rotate 270° side by side](screenshot:rotate-pages-1)

## Good to know

- You can also right-click a page and choose **Rotate page**.
- Comments and marks turn with their page.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
