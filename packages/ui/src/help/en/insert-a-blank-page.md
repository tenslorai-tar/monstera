Insert blank pages adds empty pages, each the same size as the page beside it. A dialog asks how many and where; it opens on one page after the page you are on.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Blank page** in the **Pages** group. In a narrower window it is under the group's **More**.
2. Or right-click the page and choose **Insert blank pages…**.
3. In **How many pages**, type a number from 1 to 100.
4. In **Where**, choose **Before** or **After**, then **The first page**, **The last page** or **Page** and its number.
5. Choose **Insert**.

![The Pages group's More menu with Insert blank pages…](screenshot:insert-a-blank-page-1)

## Good to know

- All the pages go in together, so one **Ctrl+Z** takes them all out again.
- With pages ticked in Organize, the dialog opens after the last ticked page.
