When no document is open, Monstera shows the start screen. From here you can open a PDF, jump straight to a kind of task, reopen a recent file, or open Settings.

## Steps

1. To open a file, choose **Open PDF…** or drop a PDF anywhere in the window.
2. To start a particular task, choose one of the six cards: **Annotate & mark up**, **Fill & create forms**, **OCR scanned pages**, **Split & merge**, **Encrypt & sign** or **Export anywhere**. Each card asks you for a PDF, opens it, and then shows the matching section of the rail (for example **Split & merge** shows **Organize**).
3. To reopen something you had before, choose it under **Recent**.
4. At the foot of the screen you will find **Settings**, **About** and **Help centre**.

![The start screen showing the Open PDF… button, the six task cards, and the Recent list](screenshot:start-screen-1)

## Good to know

- Press **F1** anywhere for help: on the start screen it opens the Help centre on the articles for getting started.
- The diagnostics log is in the **Help** menu, as **Reveal diagnostics log**.
- If Monstera closed unexpectedly last time, the start screen offers to reopen the documents that were open. See "Reopen documents after a crash".
- The start screen appears again when you close the last tab.
