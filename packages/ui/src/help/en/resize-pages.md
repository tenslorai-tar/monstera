Resize changes the paper size of pages and scales what is on them to fit, keeping its proportions.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Resize pages…** in the **Adjust** group.
2. Choose a size: **A3**, **A4**, **A5**, **Letter**, **Legal** or **Tabloid**, or type **Width (points)** and **Height (points)**.
3. Choose **This page** or **All pages**, then **Resize**.

![The Resize pages window with the paper sizes and This page / All pages](screenshot:resize-pages-1)

## Good to know

- Content is scaled to fit and centred. A turned page takes the size as you see it on screen.
- Sizes are in points (72 points to the inch), up to 14,400.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
