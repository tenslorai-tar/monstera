Insert images adds each picture as a page of its own, sized to the picture. A dialog asks where the pages go first; it opens after the page you are on.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Insert images…** in the **Pages** group. In a narrower window it is under the group's **More**.
2. In **Where**, choose **Before** or **After**, then **The first page**, **The last page** or **Page** and its number.
3. Choose **Choose images…** and pick one or more JPEG or PNG files.
4. Choose where to save the pictures as a PDF. Monstera opens that PDF as a tab and puts its pages into your document.

![The Pages group's More menu with Insert images…](screenshot:insert-an-image-as-a-page-1)

## Good to know

- The pictures become pages in the order of their file names, so *scan 2* comes before *scan 10*.
- Each new page is its picture's own size. A very large picture is scaled down to fit the largest page a PDF can have.
- A picture that cannot be made a page is left out and named in a message; the others still go in.
- All the pages go in together, so one **Ctrl+Z** takes them all out again.
- To put a picture on an existing page instead, see "Place a picture or stamp on a page".
- To make a whole new PDF from several pictures, see "Make a PDF from pictures".
