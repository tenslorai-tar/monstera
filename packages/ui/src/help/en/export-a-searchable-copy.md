**Export a searchable copy** recognises the text on every scanned page and then saves a copy of the document.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Tools**, then **Searchable copy** in the **OCR** group (**Export a searchable copy**).
2. Choose the language and start recognition, as for "Make scanned pages searchable".
3. When it finishes, choose where to save the copy.

![The OCR group with Searchable copy](screenshot:export-a-searchable-copy-1)

## Good to know

- The recognised text is also added to the open document; undo it page by page with **Ctrl+Z** if you do not want it there.
- Pages that already had text, and your comments, fields and bookmarks, are kept as they are.
- If you stop recognition early, no copy is saved.
