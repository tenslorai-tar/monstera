The loupe shows a sharp, enlarged view of the part of the page under the pointer, without changing the zoom.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Tools**, then **Loupe** in the **Display** group.
2. Move the pointer over the page.
3. Choose **Loupe** again to turn it off.

![The loupe enlarging small print under the pointer](screenshot:loupe-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcut: **Ctrl+Shift+L**.
- The loupe magnifies twice the current zoom. The amount cannot be changed yet.
- It follows the mouse, so it is not available from the keyboard; use zoom instead.
