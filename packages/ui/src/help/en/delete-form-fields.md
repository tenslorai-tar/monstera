You can remove a field completely, not just empty it.

## Steps

1. In the document panel, choose the **Forms** tab.
2. On the field's row, choose **Delete this field**.

![A Forms tab row with the Delete this field button](screenshot:delete-form-fields-1)

## Good to know

- You can delete fields that are read-only or cannot be filled, too.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
- To keep what is written in the fields but remove the form, see "Flatten a form".
