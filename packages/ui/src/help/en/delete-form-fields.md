You can remove a field completely, not just empty it.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Forms**, then **Edit fields**. Every field shows as a box with its name.
2. Click the field, or drag a box around several.
3. Press **Delete**, or right-click and choose **Delete selection**.

You can also select a field in the **Fields list** and press **Delete** there.

![A selected field in Edit fields](screenshot:delete-form-fields-1)

## Good to know

- Deleting several fields at once asks first.
- You can delete fields that are read-only or cannot be filled, too.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
- To keep what is written in the fields but remove the form, see "Flatten a form".
