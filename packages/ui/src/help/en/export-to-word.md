Export to Word turns the document's text into a Word (.docx) file you can edit.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Home**, then **Word…** in the **Export** group.
2. In **What to keep**, choose one:
   - **Text and its fonts — editable, flows like a normal document**
   - **The page layout — each line where it sits on the page**
   - **Just the words**
3. Choose **Choose where to save…** and save the file.

![The Export to Word window with the three What to keep choices](screenshot:export-to-word-1)

## Good to know

- Pictures are not carried into the Word file in any of the three choices.
- Scanned pages have no text until it is recognised; see "Make scanned pages searchable".
- The document itself is not changed.
