Share hands the document, as it is now, to the Windows Share window, where you pick your mail app or another app to send it with.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Home**, then **Share…** in the **Export** group (its full name is **Email…**).
2. In the Windows Share window, choose the app to send it with, for example your mail app.
3. Finish sending in that app.

![The Windows Share window opened from Monstera, showing the document name](screenshot:email-a-document-1)

## Good to know

- The file is shared with your changes included, under the document's name.
- Monstera only hands the file over; whether it is sent is up to the app you pick.
- If the Share window cannot open, use **Save a copy…** and attach the file from your mail app.
