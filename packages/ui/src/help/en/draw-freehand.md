Freehand lets you draw lines on a page as you would with a pen.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**, then **Freehand** in the **Markup** group.
2. Press and drag on the page to draw a stroke. Each stroke is saved as its own mark.

![A freehand circle drawn around a paragraph](screenshot:draw-freehand-1)

## Good to know

- Colour, opacity and line width come from the **Properties** tab.
- To sign a document with a drawn signature that proves who signed, see "Add a visible signature".
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
