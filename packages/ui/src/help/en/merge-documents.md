Merge adds every page of another document to the end of the one you are working on.

## Steps

1. Open both documents, so each has a tab.
2. Switch to the document that should come first.
3. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Merge…** in the **Combine** group.
4. In **Document to merge in**, choose the other document, then choose **Merge**.
5. Save the result, or use **Save a copy…** to keep it as a new file.

![The Merge a document window with Document to merge in](screenshot:merge-documents-1)

## Good to know

- The other document must be open in a tab; if nothing else is open, Monstera tells you to open it first.
- Comments and marks come across with the pages. The other document is not changed.
- To put the pages somewhere other than the end, see "Insert pages from another PDF".
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
