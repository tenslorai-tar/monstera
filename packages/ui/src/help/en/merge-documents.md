Merge adds every page of another document to the one you are working on.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Merge…** in the **Combine** group.
2. In **Merge in**, choose another open document, or choose **Choose file…** to pick a PDF. A file you pick opens as its own tab.
3. Under **Place**, choose **At the start**, **At the end** or **After page**. For **After page**, type a page of this document.
4. Choose **Merge**.
5. Save the result, or use **Save a copy…** to keep it as a new file.

![The Merge a document window with a document chosen and where it goes](screenshot:merge-documents-1)

## Good to know

- Comments and marks come across with the pages. The other document is not changed.
- To take only some of the other document's pages, see "Insert pages from another PDF".
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
