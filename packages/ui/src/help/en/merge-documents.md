Merge adds every page of one or more other documents to the one you are working on, one document after another.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Merge…** in the **Combine** group.
2. Under **Merge in**, the first other open document is listed. Choose **Add a document** to list another, or **Choose file…** to pick a PDF. A file you pick opens as its own tab and is added to the list.
3. Change a document in its list, or use the arrows beside it to move it up or down. The documents go in from the top of the list down. The cross removes one.
4. Under **Place**, choose **At the start**, **At the end** or **After page**. For **After page**, type a page of this document.
5. Choose **Merge**.
6. Save the result, or use **Save a copy…** to keep it as a new file.

![The Merge documents window with documents listed and where they go](screenshot:merge-documents-1)

## Good to know

- One merge is one step: **Ctrl+Z** takes out every document it added.
- The same document can be listed more than once.
- Comments and marks come across with the pages. The other documents are not changed.
- To take only some of another document's pages, see "Insert pages from another PDF".
