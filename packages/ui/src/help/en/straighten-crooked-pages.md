A scan fed in at a slight angle comes out crooked. Straighten measures each page and turns it upright.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Straighten** in the **Adjust** group (its full name is **Straighten crooked pages**).
2. Monstera checks every page and straightens the ones that are crooked.

![The Adjust group with Straighten](screenshot:straighten-crooked-pages-1)

## Good to know

- Pages with no lines of text or ruling to measure are left alone.
- The paper size does not change.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
- For photos of paper taken at an angle, see "Straighten photographed pages".
