Swapping puts two pages in each other's place, leaving every other page where it is.

## Steps

1. Go to one of the two pages, so it is the page you are reading.
2. In the document panel, choose the **Pages** tab.
3. Hold **Shift** and click the picture of the other page. The two pages change places.

![The Pages tab with Shift held over another page's picture](screenshot:swap-two-pages-1)

## Good to know

- From the keyboard, select the other page's picture and press **Shift+Enter**.
- Shift+clicking the page you are already on does nothing.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
