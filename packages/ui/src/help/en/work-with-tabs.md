Every document you open gets a tab at the top of the window. Each tab keeps its own page, zoom and undo history.

## Steps

1. To switch documents, choose a tab.
2. To open another document, choose the **+** button (**Open another document**) at the end of the tabs.
3. To close a tab, choose its close button (×), or press **Ctrl+W**.
4. Right-click a tab for more: **Close tab**, **Close other tabs**, or **Open side by side**.

![The title bar with three document tabs, one showing the unsaved-changes dot, and the tab right-click menu open](screenshot:work-with-tabs-1)

## Good to know

- A dot on a tab means that document has unsaved changes. The window's title (in the taskbar and Alt+Tab) shows the document's name, with a dot (●) when it has unsaved changes.
- Closing a changed document asks whether to save. See "Close a document that has unsaved changes".
- **Open side by side** shows the tab's document next to the one you are reading. See "Compare two documents side by side".
