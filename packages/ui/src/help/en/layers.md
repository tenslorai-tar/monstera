Some PDFs, such as technical drawings and maps, have layers that can be shown or hidden. The **Layers** tab lists them.

## Steps

1. In the document panel, choose the **Layers** tab.
2. Tick or untick a layer to show or hide it.

![The Layers tab with two layers, one ticked and one unticked](screenshot:layers-1)

## Good to know

- Showing or hiding a layer is a change to the document: once saved, other PDF readers open it the same way. Undo puts it back.
- If the document has no layers, the tab says so.
- You can add a page from another document as a new layer; see "Import a page as a layer".
