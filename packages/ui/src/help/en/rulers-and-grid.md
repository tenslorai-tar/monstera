Rulers run along the top and left of the page. The grid draws lines over the page at the rulers' main marks, which helps you line up marks and shapes.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Tools**. In the **Display** group, choose **Show rulers** and/or **Show grid**.
2. Choose them again to hide them.
3. To change the unit, open **Settings**, **Viewing** page, **Ruler unit**, and choose **Points**, **Millimetres**, **Centimetres**, **Metres**, **Inches** or **Feet**.

![A page with rulers along the top and left and the grid shown](screenshot:rulers-and-grid-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcuts: **Ctrl+R** for rulers, **Ctrl+G** for the grid.
- The ruler unit is also used by the measuring tools' readings.
- Rulers and grid are only on screen; they are never added to the document.
