Dim pages shows each page with light text on a dark background. It changes only what you see; the document itself is not changed.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Home**, then **Dim pages** in the **Display** group (also in **Tools**, **Display** group).
2. Choose it again to turn it off.

![A page shown with Dim pages turned on](screenshot:dark-page-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcut: **Ctrl+Shift+D**.
- This is separate from the window's theme. To make the window itself dark, see "Change the theme and accent colour".
- Colours keep their hue, so links stay blue.
