**Select annotations** lets you pick up marks you or others have added and change where they are and how big they are.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**, then **Select** in the **Markup** group (its full name is **Select annotations**). It is also in **Home**, **Quick tools**, and on the floating toolbar.
2. Click a mark to select it, or drag a box to select every mark it touches.
3. Drag a selected mark to move it; drag its handles to resize it.
4. Use the arrow keys to nudge it; hold **Shift** for bigger steps.
5. Press **Delete** to remove the selection.
6. To copy marks, right-click and choose **Copy**. Then right-click a page (in this or another document) and choose **Paste annotations**, or press **Ctrl+V**.

![Two selected annotations with their handles](screenshot:select-annotations-1)

## Good to know

- The **Properties** tab shows the selected marks' colour and other details; see "Change how annotations look".
- Picture stamps and some marks from other programs cannot be copied. Comments, shapes, drawings and highlights can.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
