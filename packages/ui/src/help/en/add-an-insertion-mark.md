An insertion mark is the small ^ symbol proofreaders use to show where something should be added.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**, then **Insertion mark** in the **Markup** group.
2. Click the spot in the text.

![An insertion mark between two words](screenshot:add-an-insertion-mark-1)

## Good to know

- The mark carries no text. To say what should be inserted, add a reply: right-click it and choose **Reply…**.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
