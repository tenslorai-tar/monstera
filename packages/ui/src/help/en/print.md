Print sends the document, with its comments and marks, to a printer.

## Steps

1. Press **Ctrl+P**, or in the rail choose **Home**, then **Print…** in the **File** group.
2. In **Pages**, keep **Every page**, or choose **Select pages** and type them, for example 1-3, 5.
3. In **Print quality**, choose **Draft** (150 dots per inch), **Standard** (300) or **High** (up to 600; a very large page is printed at less).
4. Choose **Choose a printer…**. The Windows print window opens on the pages you chose: pick the printer and the number of copies there. You can still change the pages in that window, and what it shows is what prints. If you chose more separate pages than that window can list, it offers no page choice, and the pages you chose print.

![The Print window with the Pages and Print quality choices](screenshot:print-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcut: **Ctrl+P**.
- Very large pages are printed at a lower quality than you chose, to keep the job a manageable size.
- If the printer refuses the job, Monstera cancels it and tells you. Check the printer and print again.
- To make a PDF instead of paper, you can choose **Microsoft Print to PDF** as the printer, though **Save a copy…** keeps the document as it is.
