Add page background puts a soft colour behind everything on a page, like tinted paper.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Background** in the **Marks** group (its full name is **Add page background**).
2. Choose a colour. Cream is chosen when the window opens; the last button opens a picker for any other colour.
3. Under **Pages**, choose the page you are on (or the pages you selected), or **All pages**.
4. Choose **Add background**.

![A page with the cream background, and the Background button in the Marks group](screenshot:page-background-1)

## Good to know

- The background goes behind the text and pictures, so nothing is covered.
- Pale colours keep dark text easy to read.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
