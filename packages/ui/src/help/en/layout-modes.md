Monstera has three layouts. **Ribbon** shows every tool. **Studio** keeps a compact strip and opens the tools only when you pick a section. **Focus** hides everything but the document.

## Steps

1. In the title bar, use the **Layout** switch and choose **Ribbon**, **Studio** or **Focus**.
2. In **Studio**, choose a section in the rail to open its tools; they close again once you pick one, press **Esc** or click elsewhere.
3. To leave **Focus**, press **Esc**. A note at the edge of the window reminds you of the key.

![The Layout switch in the title bar with Ribbon, Studio and Focus](screenshot:layout-modes-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcut: **Ctrl+Shift+F** enters Focus; **Esc** leaves it and returns to the layout you had.
- Your choice is remembered. You can also set it in **Settings**, **Appearance** page, **Layout**.
- In Focus, **Ctrl+K** still finds any tool.
