These shapes are made of several points you click one by one. A cloud is a polygon with a scalloped edge, often used to mark changes on drawings.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**. In the **Shapes** group, choose **Polygon**, **Connected lines** or **Cloud**.
2. Click each corner in turn.
3. To finish, double-click on the last corner, or press **Enter**. For a polygon or cloud you can also click the first corner again to close the shape.
4. **Esc** also finishes and keeps what you drew. If there are too few corners to make the shape (three for a polygon or cloud, two for connected lines), **Esc** removes it instead.

![A cloud drawn around part of a drawing](screenshot:draw-polygons-and-clouds-1)

## Good to know

- **Connected lines** stays open, so clicking the first point does not close it; double-click or press **Enter** to finish.
- Pressing **Enter** with too few corners keeps the drawing going, so you can add the rest.
- Colour, opacity and line width come from the **Properties** tab.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
