Extract pages writes a copy of the pages you choose into a new PDF. This document is not changed.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Extract pages…** in the **Pages** group. Or right-click a page and choose **Extract pages…**.
2. In **Pages to extract**, type the pages, for example `1-3, 5`.
3. Choose **Extract to a new PDF**, then choose where to save the file.
4. A message says **Pages saved**.

![The Extract pages window with Pages to extract filled in](screenshot:extract-pages-1)

## Good to know

- The new file keeps the pages' comments and form fields.
- To cut a document into several files at once, see "Split a document into several PDFs".
