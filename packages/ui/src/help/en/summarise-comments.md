When a document has many comments, the assistant can summarise them for you, citing the page of each point.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Review**, then **Summarise comments** in the **AI** group.
2. The assistant opens and asks about all the comments in the document. The line under the question says how many comments were sent.
3. Choose **Go to page …** in the answer to jump to a comment's page.

![The assistant's summary of comments with page links](screenshot:summarise-comments-1)

## Good to know

- You need a key for an AI provider; see "Get and add keys for AI and online reading services". The provider bills you directly.
- Only the comments' words are sent, not the rest of the document.
- If the list is too long to send whole, the first comments are sent and the line says so.
