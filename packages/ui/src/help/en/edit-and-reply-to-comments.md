You can change what a comment says, or reply to it so the conversation stays with the mark.

## Steps

1. Right-click the comment or mark on the page.
2. To change its words, choose **Edit comment…**, change the text, and choose **Save comment**.
3. To answer it, choose **Reply…**, type **Your reply**, and choose **Post reply**.

You can also select a mark and use **Reply** at the foot of the **Properties** tab.

![The Reply window over a note](screenshot:edit-and-reply-to-comments-1)

## Good to know

- A comment cannot be emptied; to remove it, delete the mark instead.
- Replies are saved the standard way, so other PDF readers show them as replies.
- To have the AI assistant suggest a reply, right-click the comment and choose **Draft a reply with AI**.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
