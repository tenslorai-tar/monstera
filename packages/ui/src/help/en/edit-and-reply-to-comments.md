You can change what a comment says, or reply to it so the conversation stays with the mark.

## Steps

1. Right-click the comment or mark on the page.
2. To change its words, choose **Edit comment…**. A small box opens next to the mark, holding what it says. Change the text.
3. To answer it, choose **Reply…**. An empty box named **Your reply** opens next to the mark. Type your reply.
4. Click elsewhere on the page, press **Esc**, or press **Ctrl+Enter** to finish. **Enter** starts a new line.

You can also select a mark and use **Reply** at the foot of the **Properties** tab, or change its comment there.

![The Your reply box next to a note, with a reply typed](screenshot:edit-and-reply-to-comments-1)

## Good to know

- A comment cannot be emptied; to remove it, delete the mark instead. A box left empty, or left as it was, changes nothing.
- If the text is too long for one comment, the box says so under it and stays open until it is shorter.
- Replies are saved the standard way, so other PDF readers show them as replies.
- To have the AI assistant suggest a reply, right-click the comment and choose **Draft a reply with AI**.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
