Everything drawn on a page (text, shapes, pictures) is a separate object. **Edit object** outlines them on the page so you can change one where it is.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Edit**, then **Edit object** in the **Text** group. It starts with **Edit all objects**, or with what you chose last. While it reads the page it says **Finding objects…**, then outlines the objects.
2. To work on one kind only, use the arrow beside it: **Edit all objects**, **Edit text objects**, **Edit images** or **Edit shapes**. The menu marks the one that is on.
3. Click an object to select it. Its outline turns solid, with its handles. A block of text is picked as one block, not letter by letter.
4. Then:
   - drag it to move it; the object itself moves with the pointer;
   - drag a corner handle to resize it, the opposite corner staying where it is, or a side handle to make it only wider or only taller. A block of text has corner handles only, so its words keep their shape;
   - in the **Properties** tab, on the right, change the colour of a shape or of a block of text;
   - choose **Bring forward** or **Send backward**, in the **Properties** tab or by right-clicking it, to put it in front of or behind what overlaps it;
   - or press **Delete**, or right-click it and choose **Delete selection**.
5. Press **Esc** to clear the selection, and **Esc** again, or **Edit object** again, to finish.

![The page with Edit object on: each object outlined, and the chosen one with its handles](screenshot:edit-page-objects-1)

## Good to know

- **Edit images** includes pictures you placed yourself with **Image** in the **Comment** section, as well as the pictures that are part of the page.
- A **Group** is several objects the document keeps together. It moves and resizes as one.
- Each change rewrites only the object you changed. Nothing else on the page is written again.
- If a move would put an object where the page cuts it off, nothing is changed and Monstera says so.
- Pictures and groups keep their own colours; the **Properties** tab says so.
- To change the words themselves, use **Edit text on the page**.
- Every change can be undone with **Ctrl+Z**.
