Word count totals the words, characters and lines in the whole document.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Edit**, then **Word count** in the **Proofing** group.
2. Progress shows in the status bar; you can **Cancel** it.
3. The **Word count** window shows a table of **Pages**, **Words**, **Characters (with spaces)**, **Characters (no spaces)**, **Lines** and **CJK characters**.

![The Word count window](screenshot:word-count-1)

## Good to know

- Words are counted properly in languages written without spaces, such as Chinese and Japanese.
- **CJK characters** counts the Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters, without the punctuation those languages share.
- A line that holds only spaces is not counted as a line.
- Scanned pages count only once their text is recognised.
- If counting stops early, the window says the totals are incomplete.
