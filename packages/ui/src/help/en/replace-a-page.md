Replace pages removes the page you are on, or the pages you selected, and puts pages of another document in their place, in one step.

## Steps

1. Go to the page you want to replace, or select the pages in the Organize grid.
2. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Replace pages…** in the **Pages** group.
3. The window says which pages will be removed. In **Replace with**, choose another open document, or choose **Choose file…** to pick a PDF. A file you pick opens as its own tab.
4. Under **Pages**, choose which of its pages go in. The window starts on as many pages as you are replacing, so the document keeps its length.
5. Choose **Replace page**. When you replace more than one, the button says how many.

![The Replace pages window naming the page to be removed](screenshot:replace-a-page-1)

## Good to know

- Pages that are not next to each other are each swapped for one page, in order, so choose as many pages as you are replacing.
- Pages next to each other can be swapped for any number of pages.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z** puts the original pages back.
