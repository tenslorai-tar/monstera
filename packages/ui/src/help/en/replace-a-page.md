Replace page removes the page you are on and puts the pages of another open document in its place, in one step.

## Steps

1. Open the document holding the new page, so it has its own tab.
2. Switch back and go to the page you want to replace.
3. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Replace page…** in the **Pages** group.
4. The window says which page will be removed. In **Replace it with**, choose the other document.
5. Choose **Replace page**.

![The Replace page window naming the page to be removed](screenshot:replace-a-page-1)

## Good to know

- All pages of the chosen document are put in. Use a one-page document to replace one page with one page.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z** puts the original page back.
