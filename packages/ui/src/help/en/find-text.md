Find shows every place a word or phrase appears and highlights it on the page.

## Steps

1. Press **Ctrl+F**, or in the rail choose **Edit**, then **Find** in the **Find** group. The **Search** tab of the document panel opens.
2. Type what you are looking for in **Find on this page** and choose **Search this page**.
3. To search every page, turn on **Search all pages**. Progress shows as it goes; **Cancel** stops it.
4. Use **Next match** and **Previous match** to step through the results. **Match … of …** (for example "Match 3 of 12") tells you where you are.
5. Narrow the search with **Match case**, **Whole word** or **Regular expression**.

![The Search tab with a word typed, Search all pages on, and the list of matches](screenshot:find-text-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcut: **Ctrl+F**.
- You can also select text on the page, right-click it and choose **Search for this**.
- A phrase that runs onto the next line is still found. Words split by a hyphen at the end of a line are not found as one word.
- Scanned pages can be searched after their text is recognised.
- To change words everywhere, see "Find and replace text".
