Find duplicate pages lists groups of pages that are exact copies, and can remove the extra copies for you.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**. In the **Pages** group, choose **More**, then **Find duplicate pages…**.
2. Look at the list of groups, for example "Pages 2, 7".
3. To remove the extra copies, choose **Remove … duplicate page(s)**. The button says how many pages will go.

![The Duplicate pages window listing a group and the Remove button](screenshot:find-duplicate-pages-1)

## Good to know

- Pages are compared by what is drawn on them. Comments and form entries are not compared.
- Pages that look alike but were made separately are not listed; only true copies are.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
