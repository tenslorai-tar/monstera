Delete duplicate pages lists groups of pages that are exact copies, and deletes the extra copies when you ask.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Delete duplicates** in the **Pages** group (its full name is **Delete duplicate pages…**). Unless the window is very wide, it is under the group's **More**.
2. Look at the list of groups, for example "Pages 2, 7". The first page of each group stays; the others are the copies.
3. To delete the extra copies, choose **Delete … duplicate pages**. The button says how many pages will go. When none were found, the window says so and offers only **Close**.

![The Delete duplicate pages window listing a group and the Delete button](screenshot:find-duplicate-pages-1)

## Good to know

- Pages are compared by what is drawn on them. Comments and form entries are not compared.
- Pages that look alike but were made separately are not listed; only true copies are.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
