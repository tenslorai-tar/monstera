The panel on the right has three tabs. **Properties** shows and changes how annotations look. **Assistant** is where you ask the AI assistant about your document. **Spelling** lists the words the spell check questions.

## Steps

1. Choose **Properties**, **Assistant** or **Spelling** at the top of the panel.
2. Drag the panel's edge to change its width.
3. To hide it, choose **Collapse the properties panel**. To bring it back, choose **Show the properties panel** at the right edge.

![The right panel with the Properties and Assistant tabs](screenshot:properties-panel-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcut: **Ctrl+Shift+J** shows or hides the panel.
- Its width and open tab are remembered, separately from the left panel.
- See "Change how annotations look" and "Ask the AI assistant about a document".
