**Erase annotation** removes a whole comment, highlight, drawing or shape with one click.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**, then **Erase annotation** in the **Markup** group.
2. Click the mark to remove. If marks overlap, the one on top goes.

![The Erase annotation tool about to remove a highlight](screenshot:erase-annotations-1)

## Good to know

- It removes whole marks; it does not rub out part of a drawing.
- A click anywhere inside a mark's surrounding box counts, so a click near a diagonal line can remove it.
- It works on marks that came with the document as well as your own.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**. You can also remove marks from the **Comments** tab or with **Select annotations** and **Delete**.
