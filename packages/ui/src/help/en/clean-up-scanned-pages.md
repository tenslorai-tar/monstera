**Clean up scanned pages** makes dull or greyish scans easier to read by turning them to greyscale and stretching the dark and light tones apart.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Tools**, then **Clean up** in the **OCR** group (**Clean up scanned pages**).
2. The **Clean up** window says how many scanned pages were cleaned up.

![A scanned page before and after Clean up](screenshot:clean-up-scanned-pages-1)

## Good to know

- Only scanned pages (pages that are pictures) are changed. Pages with text are left alone.
- Cleaned pages become greyscale.
- Some kinds of scanned picture cannot be changed and are skipped.
- One **Ctrl+Z** undoes the whole clean-up.
