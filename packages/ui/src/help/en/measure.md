The measuring tools draw a line or shape and label it with its length or area. They are useful on plans and technical drawings.

## Steps

1. First set the scale: open **Settings**, **Editing defaults** page. Set **Measurement scale, per point** (how much one point on the page stands for) and **Measurement unit**.
2. In the rail, choose **Comment**. In the **Measure** group, choose **Measure distance**, **Measure area** or **Measure perimeter**.
3. For distance, drag from one end to the other. For area and perimeter, click each corner and double-click to finish.
4. The measurement is written next to the shape.

![A distance measurement drawn along a wall on a floor plan, with its label](screenshot:measure-1)

## Good to know

- Without a scale, measurements are in points (72 points to the inch).
- The scale is a setting, not part of each document; change it when you move to a drawing with a different scale.
- Measurements are saved in a form other PDF readers understand.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
