The measuring tools draw a line or shape and label it with its length or area. They are useful on plans and technical drawings.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**. In the **Measure** group, choose **Distance**, **Area** or **Perimeter**.
2. In the **Properties** tab, choose the **Measurement unit**. For a drawing made to a scale, type it in **Drawing scale, 1 to**: 100 for a plan drawn at 1:100.
3. For distance, drag from one end to the other. For area and perimeter, click each corner, then double-click on the last one or press **Enter** to finish. An area also closes when you click its first corner again. **Esc** finishes and keeps the measurement too.
4. While you draw, the reading so far is shown beside the pointer. When you finish, it is written on the shape: along a distance, in the middle of an area, and beside a perimeter's longest side.

![A distance measurement drawn along a wall on a floor plan, with its label](screenshot:measure-1)

## Good to know

- With a drawing scale of 1, a measurement is the length on the page itself, in inches unless you choose another unit.
- The unit and the scale are settings, not part of each document; change them when you move to a drawing with a different scale. They are also on the **Settings** window's **Editing defaults** page.
- To change a measurement, choose **Select** and click it. Drag an end of a distance, or a corner of an area or perimeter, to move that point; drag the middle to move the whole shape. The reading and its comment are worked out again each time.
- A measurement keeps the scale it was drawn with. Changing the unit or the scale changes the next one.
- Measurements are saved in a form other PDF readers understand.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
