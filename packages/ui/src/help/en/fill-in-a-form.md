Fill a form where it is on the page, or from the **Forms** tab, which lists every field in the document with a control to fill it in.

## Steps

1. On the page, click a field. A text field opens for typing over itself: type, then press **Enter** or click elsewhere, or press **Esc** to leave it as it was. In a field with several lines, **Enter** starts a new line, and clicking elsewhere finishes.
2. Click a tick box to tick or untick it, and click an option to choose it; clicking the chosen option clears it. Click a dropdown or a list to choose from it.
3. Or, in the document panel, choose the **Forms** tab. (Or in the rail choose **Forms**, then **Fields list** in the **Fields** group; in a narrower window it is under the group's **More**.) Each row shows the field's name, its kind and its page. Choose **Go to page …** to see it on the page.
4. Fill it in there: type in a text field and press **Enter** or move away; tick a **Tick box** or **Option**; pick from a **Dropdown** or **List**.
5. Save the document to keep what you entered.

![The Forms tab listing fields with their controls](screenshot:fill-in-a-form-1)

## Good to know

- A field the document marks as read-only says **The document marks this field read-only.** in the Forms tab, and clicking it on the page does nothing. Signature fields and buttons cannot be filled here.
- A list field that holds several choices at once is shown but cannot be changed here.
- Every field is listed, however large the form.
- A form with no fields at all? Try "Find fields on a flat form", or type on it with the Typewriter.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
