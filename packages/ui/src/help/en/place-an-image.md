You can place a picture, such as a logo or a scanned stamp, onto a page. It can be moved, resized or deleted afterwards.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**, then **Image** in the **Stamps** group (its full name is **Place an image**).
2. Drag a box on the page where the picture should go.
3. Pick a PNG or JPEG file.

![A logo picture placed in the corner of a page, and the Stamps group](screenshot:place-an-image-1)

## Good to know

- To put the same picture in the same place on every page, open **Settings**, **Editing defaults** page, and set **Place images on** to **Every page** before you draw the box. The default is **This page only**. Either way it is one step to undo.
- Ready-made stamps such as APPROVED or DRAFT are not included yet; use a picture of your own.
- To move, resize or delete the picture, use **Select annotations**.
- Pictures placed this way cannot be copied and pasted.
