Duplicate makes an exact copy of a page and puts it straight after the original.

## Steps

1. Go to the page you want to copy.
2. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Duplicate page** in the **Pages** group.

![The Pages group in Organize with Duplicate page](screenshot:duplicate-a-page-1)

## Good to know

- The copy includes the page's comments and marks.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
