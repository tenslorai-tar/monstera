If a document has bookmarks, Monstera can turn them into a table of contents at the start of the document, with the right page numbers.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**, then **Contents** in the **Marks** group (its full name is **Table of contents**).
2. The new page or pages appear at the front of the document.

![A table of contents page added at the front, and the Marks group's Contents button](screenshot:table-of-contents-1)

## Good to know

- The page numbers count the new contents pages too.
- If the document has no bookmarks, Monstera says there is nothing to build from and changes nothing.
- The contents page has no heading, and its text uses a standard font, so letters outside Western European alphabets may not show correctly.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
