The document panel sits to the left of the pages. It has seven tabs, one shown at a time: **Pages**, **Bookmarks**, **Comments**, **Forms**, **Layers**, **Search** and **Accessibility**.

## Steps

1. Choose a tab icon at the top of the panel to switch to it.
2. To make the panel wider or narrower, drag its edge.
3. To hide it, choose **Collapse the document panel** (the chevron). To bring it back, choose **Show the document panel** at the left edge.

![The document panel with its seven tab icons and the collapse chevron](screenshot:document-panel-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcut: **Ctrl+Shift+B** shows or hides the panel.
- The panel's width and which tab is open are remembered.
- The size of the page pictures in **Pages** is set in **Settings**, **Appearance** page, **Thumbnail size**.
