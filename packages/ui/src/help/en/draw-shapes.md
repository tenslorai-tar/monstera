Shapes help you frame, connect or point at things on a page.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**. In the **Shapes** group, choose **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Line** or **Arrow**.
2. Drag on the page. For a rectangle or ellipse, drag from one corner to the other. For a line or arrow, drag from where it starts to where it ends; an arrow's head is at the end.

![An arrow and a rectangle drawn on a page, with the Shapes group](screenshot:draw-shapes-1)

## Good to know

- Colour, opacity and line width come from the **Properties** tab.
- To move or resize a shape later, use **Select annotations**.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
