The **Comments** tab of the document panel lists every comment, highlight and shape in the document, with its kind and page.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**, then **Comments list** in the **Markup** group. Or choose the **Comments** tab in the document panel.
2. Choose a row to go to its page.
3. To remove one, choose **Remove this annotation** on its row.

![The Comments tab listing notes, highlights and a reply, with authors](screenshot:comments-list-1)

## Good to know

- Replies are marked **Reply**. Marks that were already in the document when you opened it are marked **Came with the document**.
- Each row shows the mark's author.
- Every annotation is listed, however long the review.
- Links are not listed here; they are in the **Bookmarks** tab.
