The floating toolbar is a narrow bar on the edge of the pages. It holds everyday tools such as zoom in and out, selecting, the hand, adding a note, taking a snapshot and cropping.

## Steps

1. Choose a button on the bar to use that tool. Point at a button to see its name.
2. To hide or show the bar, choose its button in the status bar's **Panels and toolbars** group, or press **Ctrl+Shift+Q**.

![The floating toolbar on the right edge of the pages](screenshot:floating-toolbar-1)

## Good to know

- The bar never covers the page; the pages move over to make room for it.
