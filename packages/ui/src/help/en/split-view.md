Split view puts two views of the same document next to each other, so you can read page 2 while looking at page 40.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Home**, then **Split view** in the **Display** group. (It is also in **Tools**, **Display** group.)
2. Scroll each pane on its own.
3. Choose **Split view** again to go back to one pane.

![A document shown in two panes with Split view turned on](screenshot:split-view-1)

## Good to know

- Shortcut: **Ctrl+Shift+E**.
- The page field, zoom and page buttons act on the pane you last clicked in.
- To put a different document in the second pane, see "Compare two documents side by side".
