Export to PowerPoint makes a presentation with one slide per page. Each slide shows the page exactly as it looks, including drawings, pictures and comments.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Tools**. In the **Convert** group, choose **More**, then **PowerPoint…**.
2. Choose where to save the file.

![The Export group's More menu with PowerPoint…](screenshot:export-to-powerpoint-1)

## Good to know

- Each slide is a picture of the page, so the text on the slides cannot be edited in PowerPoint.
- The slide size follows the first page.
- The document itself is not changed.
