Export to PowerPoint makes a presentation with one slide per page, of every page or of the pages you choose. Each slide shows the page exactly as it looks, including drawings, pictures and comments.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Home**, then **Export** in the **Export** group, and **Export to PowerPoint…**. It is also in **Tools** › **Convert** and in the **Tools** menu.
2. In **Pages**, keep **Every page**, or choose **Select pages** and type them, for example 1-3, 5.
3. Choose **Choose where to save…** and save the file.

![The Home section's Export menu with Export to PowerPoint after Word and Excel](screenshot:export-to-powerpoint-1)

## Good to know

- Each slide is a picture of the page, so the text on the slides cannot be edited in PowerPoint.
- The slide size follows the first page you export.
- The document itself is not changed.
