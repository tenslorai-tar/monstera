Monstera shows a form's fields with a light blue wash and its required fields with a red outline, so you can see where to write. It is on to begin with.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Forms**, then **Highlight fields** in the **Manage** group.
2. Choose it again to turn it back on.

You can also choose **Highlight fields** in the **View** menu, or tick it in the **Properties** tab when no field is selected.

## Good to know

- Only your screen changes. The document is not changed, and the colour is not printed or saved.
- Monstera remembers your choice for every document. It is also in **Settings**, on the **Viewing** page.
