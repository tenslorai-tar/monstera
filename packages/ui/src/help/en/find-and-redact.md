To redact a name or number everywhere it appears, let Monstera find and mark every match for you.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Protect**, then **Redact matches…** in the **Redact** group (**Mark matches for redaction**).
2. Type the text in **Find**.
3. In **Search**, choose **Every page** or **Page …**.
4. Choose **Mark matches**. Every match is marked with a redaction mark.
5. Check the marks and delete any you did not mean, then choose **Apply redactions**. See "Redact (permanently remove) content".

![The Mark matches for redaction window](screenshot:find-and-redact-1)

## Good to know

- The search ignores capitalisation.
- Nothing is removed until you apply redactions.
- A match that wraps onto the next line gets a mark on each line.
- If there are too many matches, nothing is marked and Monstera tells you.
