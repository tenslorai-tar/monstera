Page transitions are effects, such as a fade, that PDF readers play when showing the document full screen.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Organize**. In the **Adjust** group, choose **More**, then **Page transition…**.
2. Choose **None**, **Dissolve**, **Fade**, **Box** or **Blinds**.
3. Set **Duration (seconds)**, from 0 to 60.
4. Choose **This page** or **All pages**, then **Set transition**.

![The Page transition window with the effects and Duration](screenshot:page-transitions-1)

## Good to know

- Transitions show in PDF readers' full-screen reading.
- **None** removes any visible effect.
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
