You can use your computer's camera to photograph documents and make them into a PDF.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Tools**, then **From camera…** in the **Create** group (**New PDF from camera…**).
2. Hold the page in front of the camera and choose **Take picture**. Repeat for each page; **Pictures taken: …** counts them.
3. Choose **Make PDF**, then choose where to save it. It opens in a new tab.

![The Take pictures window with the camera view and the Take picture and Make PDF buttons](screenshot:pdf-from-camera-1)

## Good to know

- The camera is on only while this window is open.
- If Monstera is not allowed to use the camera, allow it in the Windows camera privacy settings.
- To square up a photo taken at an angle afterwards, see "Straighten photographed pages".
