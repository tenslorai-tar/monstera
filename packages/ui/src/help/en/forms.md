A form is a PDF with places to type, tick and choose. Monstera fills them in, and it makes new ones.

## Fill a form

1. Open the form. There is no tool to choose: click a field on the page and type, or click a tick box or an option.
2. Press **Tab** to go to the next field and **Shift+Tab** to go back.
3. Or, in the rail, choose **Forms**, then **Fields list**: every field is listed by page, and you can fill each one there.
4. Save the document to keep what you entered.

See "Fill in a form" for the details.

## Make a form

1. In the rail, choose **Forms**. In the **Fields** group, choose a kind of field, such as **Text field** or **Tick box**.
2. Click the page to place it, or drag to give it a size. Monstera names it for you, and its properties open.
3. You are now in **Edit fields**: drag a field to move it, drag a handle to resize it, and press **Delete** to remove it. Press **Esc** to go back to filling.
4. Change a field's name, look and behaviour in its properties.
5. A page with printed lines and boxes and no fields? Choose **Detect…** and Monstera suggests where the fields go.

See "Create form fields", "Change a form field" and "Find fields on a flat form" for the details.

## Good to know

- **Flatten** turns the form into an ordinary page, so nobody can change it any more. See "Flatten a form".
- Undo with **Ctrl+Z**.
