Some things are easier to read from how a page looks than from its text, such as a table or a chart. The assistant can be sent a picture of the page.

## Steps

1. Open the assistant (**Ctrl+Shift+A**).
2. Open **Choose context**, choose **Picture**, and type your question, for example "Read the table on this page".
3. Press **Send**. The line under your question says **Sent a picture of page … of …**. Tables come back as tables.

![The assistant answering with a table read from a picture of the page](screenshot:ask-about-a-picture-of-the-page-1)

## Good to know

- Choose a model that can read pictures. If the chosen model cannot, the assistant says so and waits.
- A page too large to send as a picture is refused; ask about its text instead.
- The provider bills you directly; see "Get and add keys for AI and online reading services".
- To save tables to a spreadsheet, see "Export tables to Excel".
