Snapshot saves any rectangle of a page, including the comments on it, as a PNG picture file.

## Steps

1. In the rail, choose **Comment**, then **Snapshot** in the **Markup** group (**Snapshot a region**). It is also on the Float bar.
2. Drag a box over the area.
3. Choose where to save the PNG file.

![A box being dragged over a chart with the Snapshot tool](screenshot:snapshot-a-region-1)

## Good to know

- The document is not changed.
- The picture is made at a fixed sharpness (about twice the page's normal size); this cannot be changed yet.
- To save whole pages as pictures, see "Save pages as pictures".
