Monstera shows pages one after another by default. You can instead see a single page at a time, or pages in facing pairs.

## Steps

1. In the menu bar, choose **View**.
2. Under **Page layout**, choose **Single page**, **Continuous**, **Facing** or **Continuous facing**.

The same choices are in the **Display** group of the **Home** tab, and on the **Viewing** page of **Settings**, under **Page layout**. They are one setting: a choice made in one place shows in the others.

![The Viewing page of Settings with the Page layout choice open](screenshot:page-layout-1)

## Good to know

- **Single page** shows only the page you are on. Scroll past its end with the mouse wheel to turn to the next page, or past its top to go back one. Going to a page by number, a bookmark or a search result shows that page.
- **Facing** shows one pair of pages at a time, like an open book. Next page and the mouse wheel turn to the next pair. Going to a page shows the pair it is in, whichever side it is on.
- **Continuous facing** shows the pairs one after another, and scrolls.
- **Show cover page** puts the first page alone, so pages 2 and 3 face each other as in a printed book. It applies to both facing layouts and is off at first.
- **Fit width** and **Fit page** fit both pages of a pair to the window. A document with only one page fits that page alone. A page left without a partner, such as the last page of an odd count or the cover page, keeps the size of a pair, so the zoom does not change as you read on.
- The layout applies to every document, and to both sides of a comparison. Split view always shows one page in each half. It never changes the document.
- A document that says it reads right to left, such as a book in Arabic or Hebrew, pairs its pages from the right: the earlier page of each pair stands on the right, and the cover page stands alone on the left. Every other document pairs from the left, with the cover page alone on the right.
