# Monstera PDF Editor — Trademark Policy

Monstera PDF Editor is free, open-source software. Its **code** is licensed under the GNU
Affero General Public License, version 3 or later, and nothing in this policy limits the rights
that licence gives you.

This policy is about something else: **the name and the logos**. Tenslor Inc. publishes
Monstera PDF Editor and treats its name and logos as trademarks. They tell people that a copy
of Monstera PDF Editor comes from us. If anyone could put them on any build, nobody could tell
an official copy from a modified one, and that matters for software that opens people's
private documents.

So you are free to use, change and share the code. You can talk about the project by name
whenever you like. But if you ship something different, it has to have a different name and a
different logo.

## 1. What this policy covers

**The name:** "Monstera PDF Editor".

**The logo**, kept in the project's repository under `assets/brand/`:

| Logo | What it looks like | Master file | Files made from it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Monstera logo** | A green document shape with a folded top corner, holding a white circle with a green leaf sprig | `assets/brand/monstera_logo.png` | `assets/brand/logo-256.png`, `assets/brand/logo-hero.png`, `assets/brand/logo-hero@2x.png`, `assets/brand/logo-title.png`, `assets/brand/logo.ico`, and the Microsoft Store's tiles and icons |

The policy also covers the earlier versions of the logo kept in the repository's history (among
them the same shape with the word "Monstera" beneath the circle), any version of these at a
different size, in a different file format or in different colours, and anything designed to look
confusingly like them.

We call these, together, **"the Marks"**.

## 2. What you can do without asking

Please go ahead with any of the following. You do not need to write to us.

- **Talk about the project.** Use the name "Monstera PDF Editor" in articles, reviews,
  blog posts, videos, tutorials, books, talks, course material and comparisons, as long as
  what you say is accurate.
- **Say what your work does with it.** For example: "works with Monstera PDF Editor",
  "a plugin for Monstera PDF Editor", "compatible with Monstera PDF Editor". Keep the name
  in plain text, and do not make it look like part of your own product's name or logo.
- **Show the real thing.** Use screenshots of the unmodified application, and show the logos
  unchanged to point people to the official project or to its Microsoft Store listing.
- **Pass on official copies.** You can share an unmodified official copy of Monstera PDF Editor
  under its own name, as long as you meet the obligations of the code licence. You can also say
  truthfully that it is unmodified.
- **Describe where your fork came from.** If you build your own software from our code, you
  can say in its description, "based on Monstera PDF Editor" or "a fork of Monstera PDF
  Editor". This is allowed as a statement of origin in a sentence, not as your product's name
  (see section 4).
- **Point to the source.** Use the name to refer to the project's source repository, for
  example in a link or a citation.

Wherever you use the Marks, please do not suggest that Tenslor Inc. made, endorses or supports
your product, service or event when it does not.

## 4. If you distribute a modified version or a fork

The code licence lets you change the software and share your changes, and we want you to be
able to. The one thing we ask is that people can tell your build from ours. So before you
distribute a modified version:

1. **Give it a different name**, one that is not confusingly similar to "Monstera PDF Editor".
2. **Take out the logo and every file made from it**: everything in `assets/brand/` listed
   in section 1. In the application it appears as the application and file-type icon, the
   menu bar's mark, the start screen's logo and the About window's.
3. **Replace the name wherever the application shows it**: the window title, the word
   "Monstera" on the start screen, the About window, the app-store listing, and any download
   page.
4. You may, and we encourage you to, say that your software is "based on Monstera PDF Editor"
   in its description or About window, as a plain statement of where it came from (section 2).

Following these steps does not change your obligations under the code licence. You still have
to give your users the source code, keep the copyright and licence notices, and so on.

## 5. Official channels

Official builds of Monstera PDF Editor are published by Tenslor Inc. only through the
**Microsoft Store**. The website monsterapdf.com links to the Store listing and offers no
download of its own.

Any other download under the name "Monstera PDF Editor" is not an official build, unless it is
an unmodified copy passed on as section 2 allows.
