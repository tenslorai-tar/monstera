# ADR-0002 — Brand mark treatment: the composite logo is used as supplied

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-16
- **Amends:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §10.3 (start screen).
- **Supersedes:** `BUILD-PROMPT.md` Part M3's "circular leaf logo, the Monstera
  wordmark, 'PDF EDITOR' letterspaced beneath", and Part M8's interim
  placeholder step, which is now moot.

## Context

Part M8 is unambiguous that brand identity belongs to the project owner and
that **the agent never generates or improvises it**. It anticipated a logo
arriving as separable parts: a circular leaf mark for the 26 px title bar and
the 84 px start-screen hero, with the wordmark set as text beneath it.

The logo that was supplied is a **single composite**: a green document silhouette
with a folded corner, containing a white circle with a leaf sprig, with
"Monstera" set in a serif face across the bottom. 826 × 1025, 32-bit RGBA.

That is a different shape from what M3 described. Three treatments were possible
and the choice is the owner's, not the agent's, so it was put to them directly
along with the legibility tradeoff of each.

The owner's answer, on 2026-08-16: use the supplied artwork as-is, as the
official logo of the app, and treat the earlier design structure as withdrawn.

## Decision

`logo.png` as supplied is **the** Monstera logo, used as-is wherever brand
artwork appears: the start-screen hero, the title bar, the app icon, the file
association icon, and packaging.

Consequently:

1. The start screen renders the supplied artwork as its hero. **The separate
   text wordmark is removed**, because the artwork already contains the
   wordmark and rendering both would show the name twice. "PDF EDITOR"
   letterspaced beneath and the tagline "Built For The Way You Work" are
   unchanged.
2. The title bar renders the same artwork, scaled.
3. No cropped, recolored or otherwise derived variant is produced. The `.ico`
   for packaging is generated by **resizing only** — a format conversion of the
   owner's pixels, not a new design.
4. Part M8's "interim clearly-labeled placeholder" step no longer applies to the
   app logo; it remains in force for the Store listing assets and
   monsterapdf.com imagery, which are still to be supplied.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Crop the circular leaf mark out of the composite** and pair it with a text
  wordmark, matching M3 as written. This was the agent's recommendation, because
  a composite that bakes a serif wordmark into the artwork loses that wordmark
  to illegibility at 26 px, and because a mark-only variant scales across icon
  sizes far better. **Rejected by the owner**, who confirmed the new design
  supersedes the earlier structure. Recorded here because the tradeoff is real
  and a future reader will otherwise re-propose it: the title-bar rendering will
  read as a green document glyph rather than as legible type, which is
  acceptable and intended.
- **Request a separate mark-only asset** and use a labeled placeholder until it
  arrived. Rejected: it blocks the branding step on an asset the owner has
  stated is not coming, in exchange for a treatment the owner does not want.
- **Have the agent design a mark.** Never available. Part M8 forbids it, and
  that prohibition is not softened by the composite being awkward at small
  sizes.

## Consequences

- At 26 px the title-bar logo reads as a shape, not as a word. Accepted and
  intended; the document tabs beside it carry the identifying text.
- The artwork's aspect ratio is 0.806 (826 × 1025), not square. Every mount
  point must reserve a portrait box and letterbox within it rather than
  stretching to a square. A square `.ico` is produced by padding, never by
  distorting.
- The artwork is raster-only. There is no vector source, so very large
  renderings (Store listing hero images, a website banner) are limited by the
  1025 px height. If those are needed at higher resolution, a larger export
  must be requested from the owner — flagged now, in advance of Stage 10, per
  Part M8's "list the required sizes and formats well in advance".
- `logo.png` is a 737 KB PNG committed to the repository. This is compatible
  with Part J: the no-binaries rule targets **native binaries** — executables
  and libraries, which are provisioned by hash-pinned download — and the
  pre-commit guard admits it explicitly as allowlisted brand artwork under the
  5 MB ceiling.
