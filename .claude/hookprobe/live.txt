Probe write for the PostToolUse control-character reporter.

Any Write under this directory makes the hook emit a live-ness line and then
continue into the ordinary scan unchanged. A firing here certifies INVOCATION
only — that the harness ran the hook — and says nothing about whether the scan
is correct, which is what proof:reportControlCharacters is for.

This file's content is deliberately unremarkable. If the probe needed a finding
to fire, it would be no easier to run than the defect it exists to make
observable.
